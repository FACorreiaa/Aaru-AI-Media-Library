NEW: ASK FOR A ROADMAP FROM SEARCH
Type "the roadmap to watch Dune" or "the order to read Secret Wars" in Search, or in Discover's filter, and tap Ask Aaru. The owl lays out the steps, you check the titles it found, and Aaru saves the whole roadmap as a list of its own: every step, every title in order, and the ones it could not find written down so nothing is lost. Ask for the same roadmap again and you get a second list; the first stays as it was.

ALSO NEW

AARU IN SHORT
One private library for films, shows, anime, manga, books and albums. Keep what you plan to start, what you are in the middle of, and what you finished; tick episodes and chapters as you go, and see what airs this week. Bring your history from Trakt, Letterboxd, Goodreads, MyAnimeList, AniList, IMDb, Netflix, TV Time and more. Ask Aaru in your own words, typed or spoken. Nothing you keep is shown to anyone unless you choose to share it.
