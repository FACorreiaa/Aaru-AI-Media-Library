Sign in with the demo account above: on the first screen choose "Continue with email" and enter the address and the code given as the password. The account has a library, lists, a week of episodes, a friend and a group, so every tab has content.

What Aaru is: a private library for films, shows, anime, manga, books and albums. It plays nothing and sells nothing. Aaru Plus is not on sale in this version, so there is no purchase to test and no paywall is shown.

Where to look:
- Home: Up Next, the week, and three public rows from the catalog.
- A title's page: status, rating, notes, episodes, Related, and "From here".
- The owl in the top bar opens the assistant. It reads only this account's own library and the public catalog; replies are written by Anthropic's Claude through its API, as the privacy policy says.
- AI data sharing (guidelines 5.1.1(i), 5.1.2(i)): before anything of the user's goes to Anthropic, Aaru asks, on the first run or in a full-screen ask on the next open, naming what is sent and to whom. "Don't allow" keeps the owl, capture and @aaru off and the rest of Aaru working. Settings > Your data > AI data sharing changes the answer. The server refuses every AI request from an account that has not allowed it.
- Settings > Import: bring a history in from a file or a connected service.
- Settings > Export your data, and Settings > Delete account (asks to sign in again if the session is older than ten minutes). Deleting an account that signed in with Apple also revokes Aaru's Sign in with Apple tokens with Apple.

Content from other people (guideline 1.2). It exists only between friends: there is no public profile, no public feed and no way to reach a stranger.
- Friends tab: add a friend by handle or invite link. A profile has Report and Block.
- Groups: long-press (or right-click on Mac) a message for Report and Block. A blocked person's messages are hidden.
- Board: a friends-only list of posts. A post's menu has Report and Block; long-press (or right-click) a comment for the same.
- Posts, comments, group messages, names and notes another person can read are checked for slurs, explicit sexual terms and threats before they are saved; a refused one is not posted and the writer is told why.
- Reports reach the developer, who can remove content and close accounts. The terms (https://aaruapp.com/terms) set the rules.

Location is asked for only when you ask for concerts near you, in Music; it is read once, approximately, and not stored. The microphone is asked for only when you choose to speak to the assistant instead of typing.
