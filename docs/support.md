# Support

_Last updated 12 November 2026._

Aaru is made by one developer. If something is broken, missing or confusing, write and a person
will read it.

## Get in touch

- **Email:** [privacy@aaruapp.com](mailto:privacy@aaruapp.com), for anything: a bug, a question,
  your account, your data.
- **Discord:** [discord.gg/GG55YPANC](https://discord.gg/GG55YPANC), to talk with the developer
  and other people who use Aaru.

When you report a bug, say which app you are on (iPhone, iPad, Mac or the web) and what you were
doing. Do not send a password or a sign-in code: nobody at Aaru will ask for one.

## Your account

- **Sign in.** Use the same way you signed up with: Apple, Google, or a code sent to your email.
  A code that does not arrive is usually in the spam folder; ask for another after a minute.
- **Export your data.** Settings → Export your data, in the app or on the web. You get your
  library, lists, progress and history as JSON or CSV.
- **Delete your account.** Settings → Delete account, in the app or on the web. It is removed at
  once; if you signed in more than ten minutes ago, Aaru asks you to sign in again first. You
  can also write to the address above and it will be deleted for you.

## Imports

- An import never adds anything before you see what it matched and approve it.
- A row Aaru could not match is listed, so you can find the title yourself or leave it out.
- Every import can be undone afterwards, from History in Settings.

## People

- **Report** a message, a post or a profile from its menu. Reports are read by the developer.
- **Block** someone from their profile or from Friends. They are not told, and what they write
  is hidden from you.

## Rules and privacy

The [terms of use](terms.md) say what Aaru expects. The [privacy policy](privacy.md) says what
Aaru stores, who else sees any of it, and how long it is kept.
