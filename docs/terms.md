# Terms of use

_Last updated 11 November 2026. Draft: the developer has not yet approved this text._

Aaru is a private media library, with friends-only groups for watching or reading together.
These terms say what you can expect from Aaru and what Aaru expects from you. How your data is
handled is in the [privacy policy](privacy.md), which is part of these terms. If the two ever
disagree about data, the privacy policy wins.

## The short version

- You must be **13 or older**, or the age at which your country lets you agree to an online
  service on your own.
- Your library is yours. What you post to friends and groups is your responsibility.
- Don't harass people, spam, post anything illegal, or pretend to be someone else.
- You can **report** a post, a group message or a person, and **block** anyone. Reports are read
  by the developer, who can remove content or close accounts.
- Aaru comes **as it is**, with no warranty.
- You can **delete your account** in Settings whenever you like.

## Who can use Aaru

You may use Aaru if you are at least 13 years old. Where the law of your country sets a higher age
for agreeing to an online service without a parent or guardian, you must be at least that age, or
have your parent or guardian agree for you. If you are not allowed to use Aaru, don't create an
account. Aaru may close an account that it learns belongs to someone below that age.

## Your account

You sign in with Apple, Google or your email. Keep your sign-in safe: you are responsible for what
happens under your account. Give accurate details, one account per person. If you think someone
else got into your account, sign out of your other devices in Settings and write to us.

Some actions ask you to sign in again first. Deleting your account is one: it needs a sign-in
from the last 10 minutes.

## Your content

What you add to Aaru is yours: your library, ratings, notes, lists, and what you write in groups
and on Boards. You give Aaru only the permission it needs to store it, show it to the people you
chose (your friends, the members of a group) and run the service for you. Aaru does not sell it
and does not use it to advertise.

You are responsible for what you post. Group messages are stored readable on Aaru's server and are
not end-to-end encrypted; the apps say so in every group.

## What you must not do

Don't use Aaru to:

- harass, threaten, bully or target another person, or post hateful content about people for who
  they are;
- send spam, unwanted repeated invitations or friend requests, or advertising;
- post anything illegal, including content that sexualises children, content that incites
  violence, or content you have no right to share;
- pretend to be another person or organisation;
- break, probe or overload the service, get into other people's accounts, or collect other
  people's data;
- use Aaru for anything unlawful.

Aaru is friends-only by design: there is no public feed and no public profile. These rules apply
to every place where another person can read what you wrote: Boards, groups, recommendations, share
links, profiles, handles and photos.

## Reporting and blocking

You can **block** any person, which hides you from each other. You can **report** a Board post, a
group message or a person from their profile. A report carries a reason and an optional note. The
person you report or block is not told.

Reports are read by the developer, who runs Aaru. The developer may remove content, warn a person,
or limit or close an account, without notice when the harm is serious or repeated. Aaru does not
promise to reply to every report, but reads them. If you think a decision was wrong, write to us
and we will look again.

## Other people's data in Aaru

Titles, posters, episode lists, scores and where to watch come from other services: TMDB, TVmaze,
AniList, Open Library, MusicBrainz and the Cover Art Archive, ListenBrainz, Wikidata, JustWatch
(through TMDB), Ticketmaster and TheSportsDB. That data belongs to those services and their
sources. Aaru credits them and is not their owner; it is not endorsed or certified by them. Aaru
uses Trakt and MyAnimeList only if you connect them. Tickets and streams are on other sites,
under their terms.

Aaru does not host, stream or sell films, shows, books or music. It tracks them.

## Ending your account

You can stop at any time: Settings → Delete account, in the app or on the web. That removes your
library and account at once; the privacy policy says what remains in backups and for how long.

Aaru may suspend or close an account that breaks these terms, or that puts other people or the
service at risk. Where it can, it will tell you why.
